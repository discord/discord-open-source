The following entries were removed during the 2026 maintenance refresh because their recorded Discord invites returned `404 Unknown Invite` and no replacement invite could be verified from an official project source:

- Amethyst Game Engine — the upstream project describes itself as inactive.
- Buefy
- Gridcoin

These are data removals, not claims that the projects themselves no longer exist. A project can be restored through a pull request once its maintainers provide a permanent, publicly joinable Discord invite and meet the repository's acceptance criteria. Historical logo assets are retained for now to avoid deleting contributor-provided artwork without a maintainer review.
